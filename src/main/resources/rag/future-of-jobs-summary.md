# Future Of Jobs 2025 Course Summary

The World Economic Forum Future of Jobs 2025 report identifies technology,
demographic, geoeconomic, and green-transition forces as major labor-market
drivers through 2030.

Among technology trends, artificial intelligence and information processing are
expected to be among the most transformative influences on work. AI affects both
automation and augmentation: some tasks are displaced, while many roles change
as workers use AI systems for analysis, content generation, decision support,
and software development.

The course uses this summary as a small local RAG source so students can test
retrieval without embedding an entire PDF during every build.
