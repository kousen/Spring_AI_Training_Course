## Resources To Keep Up With AI

The AI ecosystem moves quickly. I recommend tracking a mix of:
1. A few high-signal newsletters
2. A few practical YouTube channels
3. Primary sources (labs, docs, papers)
4. One community feed (Reddit/Hacker News/X/LinkedIn)

Treat this as a curated menu you can adapt to your workflow and role.

## Newsletters and Blogs

- [One Useful Thing](https://www.oneusefulthing.org/) (Ethan Mollick)  
  Practical, business-friendly analysis of AI trends and implications.
- [Simon Willison's Weblog](https://simonwillison.net/)  
  Excellent technical breakdowns, links, and hands-on experiments.
- [Nate's Substack](https://www.nateliason.com/) and [AI News and Strategy Daily](https://www.youtube.com/@NateBJones)  
  Frequent practical updates and strategy framing.
- [Machine Learning Street Talk Substack](https://substack.com/@mlst)  
  Long-form technical perspectives and interviews.
- [The Batch](https://www.deeplearning.ai/the-batch/) (DeepLearning.AI)  
  Weekly summary of major AI developments.
- [Import AI](https://www.importai.com/) (Jack Clark)  
  Policy, research, and industry context from an experienced insider view.
- [Latent Space](https://www.latent.space/)  
  Strong practitioner-focused writing on building with LLMs.
- [Ben's Bites](https://www.bensbites.com/)  
  Curated daily AI news in concise format.
- [TLDR AI](https://tldr.tech/ai)  
  Brief daily AI links and summaries.
- [JVM Weekly](https://www.jvm-weekly.com/) (Artur Skowronski)  
  Java ecosystem updates, often including AI/JVM tooling.
- [Tales from the jar side](https://kenkousen.substack.com/)  
  Your course-aligned perspective on AI and Java.
